# Experiment 04: additional controlled ablations

`additional_ablation_variant_raw.csv` and
`additional_ablation_variant_summary.csv` are the authoritative source
evidence: 5 variants x 3 conditions x 10 seeds = 150 raw rows and 15 summary
groups. The `equal_weights` exploratory control remains archived; it was not
deleted simply because it is omitted from the compact paper table.

The current paper Table 15 display is separately exported under
`paper_current_table_15`. It contains the reused Full aggregate plus three
controls: `homogeneous_other` (paper label: Homogeneous current-style),
attention D=128, and direct softmax. `homogeneous_vibration` and
`equal_weights` remain supplementary evidence.

The Full row was reused from the original 10-run Stage-2 experiment. Therefore
differences between Full and new controls are descriptive, unpaired aggregate
differences; no paired SD, p-value, or significance claim is supported.
